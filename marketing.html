Built for practical field screening

The app focuses on quick measurement setup, consistent recording flow, and readable outputs. It helps users compare conditions, review trends, and export structured reports without carrying a laptop.

  • XYZ live acceleration display during setup
  • Selectable measurement duration for low-frequency improvement
  • Raw accelerometer or fused user acceleration source selection
  • High-pass and optional analysis low-pass filtering
  • Velocity spectrum and 1/3 octave charts with dynamic Y-axis scaling

Reduce tap noise

A built-in countdown starts measurement after your hand is off the device, helping reduce operator-induced disturbance.

Export and share quickly

Generate PDF reports and CSV files directly on iPhone for review, archiving, or external analysis.

Designed for comparison

Keep measurement durations and settings consistent across tests to compare floor response more efficiently.

Important note

This app is intended for engineering screening and relative comparison. Smartphone MEMS sensors have known limitations, especially in low-frequency measurement. The app should not be presented as a replacement for certified professional vibration equipment.

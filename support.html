Common Questions

Why does low-frequency data look unstable?

Smartphone MEMS sensors are limited at low frequencies. In this app, results above roughly 5 Hz are generally more reliable, while 1–5 Hz should be treated as trend reference only.

Why do I need countdown and fixed-duration measurement?

The app waits before recording to reduce tap-induced vibration. Fixed durations also improve repeatability and make PSD comparisons more consistent.

Why does PDF export take time?

The report is generated on-device and includes multiple rendered charts. Longer measurements and more data points can increase processing time.

Why does static calibration not remove all noise?

Static calibration removes offset, but it does not eliminate random sensor noise or low-frequency drift. For better results, use rigid mounting, longer measurement windows, and repeated tests.

Troubleshooting Checklist

  • Place the iPhone on a rigid, stable surface and avoid holding it by hand.
  • Use a longer duration such as 60 s or 120 s if low-frequency stability matters.
  • Run static calibration before measurement if the baseline appears shifted.
  • Start a new measurement after changing analysis low-pass settings.
  • Keep the device awake and avoid incoming interruptions during recording.
